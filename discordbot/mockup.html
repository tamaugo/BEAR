🐻 How to use BEAR
1. One channel per car. The channel topic is the car, make first:
FORD FIESTA MK7 2012 HATCHBACK 1.4 DIESEL

2. /newfolder Q184 opens a folder. Everything after it belongs to that folder.

3. Upload the part-number photos in any groups you like. Only part-number photos: every photo is priced as one part.

4. /run when the folder is done. BEAR says how many parts it found, then posts the spreadsheet. If that number is lower than you expect, a photo is missing.
Good to know
Every photo gets a reply with the part number BEAR reads: tap Correct, another reading, or Fix to type it.
Text messages are removed: car channels are photos only.
/run again re-does the whole folder.
/rename renames every photo to SKU-1, SKU-2 ... and posts them as a zip.
/quit stops whatever is running in the channel.
/status shows what BEAR is working on.
Problems also go to #bear-errors.
