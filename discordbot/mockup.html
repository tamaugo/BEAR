🐻 How to use BEAR
1. One channel per car. The channel topic is the car, make first:
FORD FIESTA MK7 2012 HATCHBACK 1.4 DIESEL

2. /newfolder Q184 opens a box. Everything after it belongs to that box.

3. Upload the photos in any groups you like.

4. Part-number photo on its own, then react 💸. Only 💸 photos are priced.

5. /run when the box is done. BEAR says how many parts it found, then posts the spreadsheet. If that number is lower than you expect, a 💸 is missing.
Good to know
Text messages are removed: car channels are photos only.
/run again re-does the whole box.
/status shows what BEAR is working on.
Problems also go to #bear-errors.
